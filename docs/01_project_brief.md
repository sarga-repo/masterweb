# 01 - Project Brief

## Project name

Sarga.co Website Revamp and Group Gateway

## Main objective

Revamp `sarga.co` into a modern, fast, mobile-friendly, SEO-ready group gateway for the Sarga ecosystem. The site must clearly introduce Sarga as a 360° sports and entertainment ecosystem and route visitors to child businesses/IPs such as Sarga Horse Sport, Sarga Motorsport, and future Sarga business units.

## Business positioning

Sarga.co should communicate that Sarga is a highly integrated sports and entertainment group combining:

- Elite horse sports
- Motorsport tracks and events
- Live entertainment and festivals
- Media rights and publication
- Ticketing journey
- Technology and ecosystem infrastructure

## Primary website role

The website is not only a company profile. It is the parent gateway for the group, brand portfolio, event campaign, publication, and lead/ticketing journey.

## Target users

1. General visitors  
   Need to understand what Sarga is and explore the ecosystem.

2. Event visitors and fans  
   Need to discover events and access ticket purchase journey through a partner ticketing flow.

3. Sponsors, partners, and media  
   Need to understand Sarga's ecosystem, publications, media assets, and contact channel.

4. Internal Sarga content team  
   Need to update content without developer involvement.

5. Potential employees  
   Need to view career information or reach recruitment links/forms.

## Initial business units / ecosystem modules

- Sarga Horse Sport
- Sarga Motorsport
- Sarga Media
- Sarga Festival
- Sarga Rising Star
- Sarga Venues
- Sarga Tech

## Success criteria

- Sarga.co clearly works as the group gateway.
- Users can easily navigate to Sarga Horse Sport and Sarga Motorsport.
- The structure can be expanded to future ecosystem pages.
- Internal team can manage content through Strapi CMS.
- Website is fast, responsive, SEO-ready, and production-hardened.
- Ticketing journey is visible and clear, without building an internal ticketing/payment engine.
- Source code, repository access, deployment guide, admin credentials, and technical documentation can be handed over to Sarga.
