# Codex Prompt 03 — Homepage

Task: build the homepage using mock data first.

Requirements:

- Follow `docs/04_page_specifications.md`.
- Follow `docs/12_visual_reference_guideline.md`.
- Review page 7 of `reference/source-pdfs/sarga_website_preview.pdf` for the brand foundation and pages 1-5 as implementation inspiration.
- Implement:
  - Hero section
  - About preview
  - 360° Ecosystem section with tabs/cards
  - News & Publications preview
  - Ticket Hub CTA
  - Newsletter section
- Visual direction:
  - Original premium art direction rather than a reconstruction of the sample homepage
  - Strong editorial hierarchy and a distinctive, high-impact hero
  - Sarga brand colors, typefaces, racing graphics, and cinematic image style from page 7
  - Purposeful motion and interaction where they improve comprehension or brand energy
  - Refined responsive compositions designed for each breakpoint, not merely stacked desktop sections
- Use mock images/placeholders until official assets are added.
- Keep content isolated in mock data so it can later be replaced by Strapi.

Acceptance criteria:

- Homepage is responsive.
- CTA buttons link to correct routes.
- Content structure maps to future CMS fields.
- Build passes.

Visual acceptance criteria:

- The homepage must feel unmistakably Sarga through the page-7 brand foundation, while its layout, navigation treatment, hero composition, cards, transitions, and footer are original.
- Pages 1-5 calibrate premium quality and brand energy; visual similarity to their exact layouts is not an acceptance criterion.
- The result should meet a premium international frontend standard: polished typography, disciplined spacing, clear interaction states, responsive art direction, accessibility, and strong performance.
